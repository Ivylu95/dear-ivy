## Preparing evidence for an assessment

When she gathers what she has noticed for an assessment (an ADHD one, say): a lifelong
pattern is dated by period ("through school", "first job"), a single event by its exact
date. Assessors look for when a pattern began and how long it has held, and a period is the
honest unit for that; a guessed exact date for a pattern reads as precision it does not have.
