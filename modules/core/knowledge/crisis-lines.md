# Crisis lines by country

_The crisis numbers a copy may give, one entry per country, each with its
source and the date it was checked (`SAF-016`). Setup copies her country's
entry into her record before her first conversation; in a crisis the copy is
read as written, never searched for. Re-checked every 3 months and when she
moves country (`SAF-017`). A copy whose country has no entry here cannot
finish setup._

**Interval:** 3 months · **Next due:** 2026-11-28

**Read** column, as in [`practices.md`](practices.md): `full` = the
operator's or government's own page was opened and read · `summary` = only a
search summary was reachable.

## Roles

| Role | What it is for |
|---|---|
| first | The one route given first when she is not safe |
| text | The fallback when talking is too much |
| later | For later, or while she waits for care |
| emergency | Danger this minute |

## No entry for her country

Setup stops. Until an entry is added, the fallback is her country's own
emergency number and [Find A Helpline](https://findahelpline.com), a directory
that verifies its listings with the providers. Never a number from memory.
