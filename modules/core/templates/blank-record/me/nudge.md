# The check-in nudge

**Off until she asks for it, and off again the moment she says so.** When it is
on, a weekly check-in that leaves a question for her also sends one line to her
phone: "Something's waiting for you in Dear." Never offered to her; only she
starts it.

## What she said

_Her words, dated, each time she turns it on or off._
