# Nutrition — the maintained evidence base

Cited reference values behind the fitness module. Nothing personal: every row says what is
known in general, never who asked or why.

**Tier** and **Read** are defined in [`modules/core/knowledge/README.md`](../../core/knowledge/README.md).
**An uncited line does not belong in this file.** A row older than 12 months is re-checked
before it is quoted. These are population reference values, not advice for one person: whether
an amount is right for her is her doctor's or a pharmacist's call.

---

## Check-ins

**Terms.** A *just-in-time adaptive intervention* (JITAI) offers help at *decision points* (here,
each hourly run), reading *tailoring variables* (time since the last meal logged, usual meal
times, what is left against her goals, when the last message went, whether she answered) through
*decision rules* that choose an *intervention option*, doing nothing among them. *Receptivity* is
whether she can and will act on a prompt then; *burden* is what each prompt costs her attention.

The current practice the nutrition update is judged by. Her own preferences, under **Updates on
Telegram** in her record, override these (defaults with overrides). Re-checked by the next development
session past the dates below.

| Practice | Why | Source | Tier | Read | Checked |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Decide at each run whether to message, with sending nothing the default | The just-in-time adaptive intervention framework: at each decision point, tailoring variables feed decision rules that pick an option, one of which is doing nothing; this keeps help timely and burden low | Nahum-Shani et al. (2018), *Annals of Behavioral Medicine* 52(6):446–462 | 3 | — | 2026-10-26 |
| Check in before a usual meal, just after one is logged, or after a long gap | Messages work through content, timing and interactivity; tailored to recent eating, not on a flat schedule (a flat weekly check-in changed nothing in a one-year programme) | PMC6534047 (2019); NCT07582536 (registered trial design) | 2 | — | 2026-10-26 |
| At most three a day, three or more hours apart, none late at night; fewer after two go unanswered, and none after three in a row until she writes or logs again | Every prompt costs attention, and receptivity falls when prompts pile up or come when a person cannot act; current trials send one or two a day. The numbers are this harness's working values, not a published cap | arXiv 2508.02817 (2025); NCT07582536 | — | — | 2026-10-26 |
