# skills/

The fitness module's skills. Each loads as `fitness:<skill>` through the
module's plugin; see [`../README.md`](../README.md).

| Folder | What it holds |
|---|---|
| [`log/`](log/SKILL.md) | What she ate, drank or took, each figure marked by its source, and the day's summary |
| [`supplements/`](supplements/SKILL.md) | Her supplement list: two views, brand and label for each, one re-check per pack |
| [`recovery/`](recovery/SKILL.md) | Her sleep, soreness and energy a day a line, and the last seven days read back |
| [`training/`](training/SKILL.md) | Her sets as weight × reps × reps left, her bodyweight, and where each lift's estimated max stands |
