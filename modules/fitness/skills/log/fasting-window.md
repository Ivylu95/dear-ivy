# The day inside a fasting window

**Read when:** with `day.md`, on a day inside a fasting window or a target over several days.

- The window's total against its target, and what is left over its remaining days. No day in
  it is described alone or as short: not "low", "light", "only", "lighter than", "very
  little", or set against a typical day or plan; each day's figures plainly beside the total.
- Each day's figures go in its file, marked `Fasting:` with the window in her words, so the
  total comes right next time.
