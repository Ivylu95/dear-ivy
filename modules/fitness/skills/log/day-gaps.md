# The day with a meal not logged

**Read when:** with `day.md`, not `missing.md`, once a usual meal's time has passed unlogged on a
tracked day.

**Missing is not nothing** (card A-100): taken as eaten, not yet logged.

- Totals stand as logged, "lunch not logged" beside them; the room left is "not counting
  lunch", never "before lunch" (that reads as lunch still to come, budgeted as nothing).
- Passed means by her time: her words ("this morning", "it's 4pm") outrank the machine's clock.
  A meal still ahead of her (dinner, at 4 pm) is never asked about or marked; breakfast
  "this morning" leaves nothing passed, so nothing is asked or marked until she says a later
  time.
- Asked once that day, lightly: "did you have lunch? a photo or a few words is enough, and
  'nothing' counts too". Her "nothing" is kept, her words, as that meal's row; silence leaves it
  "not logged". Then never raised again that day, not even as an aside ("if you didn't have
  lunch…").
- Nothing is closed, fitted or added up past it: no suggestion sized to use lunch's share, no
  "fits inside your room", no "you'd still need more protein", no "a yoghurt would cover the
  rest", no total with dinner added. Each idea gives only its own figures: "~1,435 kcal and
  75 g protein left, not counting lunch. Steamed fish, rice and greens: ~35 g protein, ~500 kcal."
- On a day marked **Fasting**, a meal outside her eating window is skipped, not missing: never
  marked, never asked about (card A-099).
