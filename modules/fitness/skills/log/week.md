# Showing the week

**Read when:** she asks how her week went, or for a weekly view, or it is her first log after a Sunday and she has asked for it weekly. Part of [`SKILL.md`](SKILL.md), whose rules all still hold, the ~ rule (card A-145) among them; one level deep, nothing further to open.

When she asks ("how was my week", "weekly view"), or once on her first log after a Sunday if
she has asked for it weekly; never pushed otherwise. On that log the week view replaces the
day's summary (this page is read instead of `day.md`): the week includes today.

- Read the last seven files in `data/fitness/days/`, today's included, **Targets** in
  `data/fitness/README.md`, and, for anything without a target, its standard: the daily need
  in `../../knowledge/practices.md` for her age and sex from `data/PROFILE.yaml`, or the label
  Daily Values there where those are not known. Days with nothing logged are named, and left
  out of the averages, never counted as zero.
- **Shape:** a table of three short columns that fits a phone without sideways scrolling, one
  emoji leading each row, ~ on estimates, cells under about 16 characters, a bar of ten (▰ done,
  ▱ to go) with the percentage; a longer note in one line under it. Average a day for
  calories and each macro, with how many days it is from and her target where she has one
  (a budget reads as room, never to fill); then the nutrients short on most days, and anything
  over a limit on any day (🟡 short, 🔴 over, ❔ unknown).
- One plain line at most on what stands out, as an observation she can take or leave; no
  scores, no streaks, no "well done".
