# Tracking is hers to switch on, day by day

**Read when:** a day has nothing logged, or on a tracked day a usual meal's time has passed with nothing logged and the day is not being shown (when it is, `day-gaps.md` holds what the view needs). Part of [`SKILL.md`](SKILL.md), whose rules all still hold; one level deep, nothing further to open.

She tracks some days and not others (card A-049). A day with nothing logged is an untracked
day: never counted as zero, never shown as a gap, never asked about afterwards. When she does
log, everything here works in full from that item on. A weekly or fasting view names its
untracked days and leaves them out.

**Missing is not nothing** (card A-100). On a day she is tracking, a usual meal whose time has
passed with nothing logged is taken as eaten and not yet logged, never as zero: the totals say
"lunch not logged", and the next update or chat asks once, lightly ("did you have lunch? a photo
or a few words is enough, and 'nothing' counts too"). **Passed means by her time, not the
machine's:** what she says or implies ("this morning", "it's 4pm") outranks the clock, which can
be hours off; a meal still ahead of her (dinner, at 4 pm) is never asked about or marked, so the
ask names only the meal behind her: "did you have lunch?", never "lunch and dinner?". Breakfast
logged "this morning" means it is still morning for her: nothing has passed, nothing is asked,
and the totals carry no "not logged" at all; lunch is asked about once, at the first reply after
she says it is past (her "it's 4pm"). Her "nothing" is kept, in her words, as
that meal's row; her silence leaves it "not logged". Once means once that day: after the ask,
it is never raised again, not as a question, not as an aside ("if you didn't have lunch…"), and
nothing is worked out as if the meal were nothing. Totals stand as logged with "lunch not
logged" beside them; the room left is marked "not counting lunch" (never "before lunch", which
reads as lunch still to come, as if it were nothing), and no suggestion is sized to use
lunch's share as if it were free. On a day marked **Fasting**, a meal outside her eating window
is skipped, not missing: never marked, never asked about (card A-099: none for a meal she skips). Nor is any figure added up past it: no "dinner takes you
to 60% of your protein", no "you'd still need more to reach 90 g", no extra item to close a
gap lunch may already have closed. While a meal is not logged, a suggestion gives only its own
protein and calories, and what is left is always "not counting lunch", in every reply that
day, the dinner ideas included.

**Worked case** (E-046). Tracking today against 1,800 kcal and 90 g protein, breakfast logged
(~270 kcal, 14 g), an apple at 4 pm (~95 kcal), lunch not logged, and she asks for dinner ideas.
Right: "So far ~365 kcal and 15 g protein, lunch not logged: ~1,435 kcal and 75 g left, not
counting lunch." Each idea then carries only its own figures, "steamed fish, rice and greens: ~35 g
protein, ~500 kcal", and the lunch question, already asked, is not asked again. Wrong: "left before lunch" (lunch is behind her, eaten, just not logged), "did you have lunch
and dinner?" at 4 pm, "fits inside your room", "you'd still need a bit more protein later", "a yoghurt would cover the
rest", or a total with dinner added: each spends lunch's share as if it were nothing.
