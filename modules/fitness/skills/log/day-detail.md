# More of the day, when she asks

**Read when:** she asks, in the chat or the update, for more than her goals: a nutrient without
a target, what is still short or over a limit, or a full breakdown. Read with
[`day.md`](day.md); part of [`SKILL.md`](SKILL.md), whose rules all hold; one level deep.

- **Anything without a target** (carbs, fat, sugar and the rest), once she has set any, only
  when she asks, in the chat or the update (card A-145), each against its standard.
- **In the chat, when asked:** the vitamins and minerals still short, and anything over an
  upper limit (once that a pharmacist or her doctor answers whether it is right for her), in
  the day's three-column shape (🟡 short, 🔴 over, ❔ unknown).
- **A full breakdown**, every nutrient, only when she asks for it, in the same three-column
  shape (🟡 short, 🔴 over, ❔ unknown).
