# Fasting

**Read when:** she says she is fasting, or sets or changes a fast or a target over several days. Part of [`SKILL.md`](SKILL.md). The day inside a window is shown by `fasting-window.md`.

- A fasting day is marked at the top of its day file (`Fasting:` her words, "16:8", "a full
  day"), and a target with a window of several days ("over 3 days", "this week") is measured
  across that window, not each day.
- The summary then shows the window's total against the window's target, and what is left
  over the days remaining in it; a light day inside a window is never shown as short on its own.
  No day in it is described alone: not "low", "light", "only", "lighter than", "very little",
  or set against a typical day or plan; each day's figures are given plainly beside the
  window's total. Each day's figures go in its file, marked `Fasting:` with the window
  in her words, so the total comes right next time.
- How she fasts is hers; whether fasting suits her medicines or a health condition is her
  doctor's: say so once, when she first sets one, about fasting in general, never a day's amount.
