# Goals she sets

**Read when:** she sets or changes a target, or asks what hers should be. Asking for one when **Targets** in `data/fitness/README.md` is empty is in `goals-ask.md`. Part of [`SKILL.md`](SKILL.md), whose rules all still hold; one level deep, nothing further to open.

- **Asked for, never assumed** (card A-043). With no target under **Targets** in
  `data/fitness/README.md`, ask in the same reply as the first item logged in a chat, as its
  one question: measure against the standard daily amounts, or set her own, and for calories, a
  budget to stay under or a target to hit? Her topic comes first: an owed profile question
  (her name) waits for a later pause (A-144), and the updates offer below waits for a reply
  with no other question. Until she answers, the standard stands. Her "not now" is kept with
  its date and asked again only after a week. If she
  asks what hers should be, the `look-it-up` rules apply, the options are laid out, and she
  picks; nothing is worked out from her body unless she gives the figures and asks.
- **Budget, target or minimum** (card A-043): when she sets calories, ask once which she means:
  a budget to stay under (losing weight, say), or a target to hit (gaining). Protein is a
  minimum unless she says otherwise. Her aim decides; it is never assumed.
- Kept under **Targets**: the target, the amount, its kind, which days (every day, training,
  rest), the window (a day, or several days for fasting), the date, and her words. A change is a new
  dated row; the old row stays, marked ended.
- **Training or rest day** is read from `data/fitness/training.md` (a session logged today, or
  her training week); if neither says, ask once, the first time a target depends on it.
