# Asking for goals

**Read when:** the first item is logged in a chat while **Targets** in `data/fitness/README.md`
is empty, unless a "not now" there is under a week old.

**Goals are asked for, never assumed** (card A-043): that reply brings one question: standard
amounts or her own, and for calories a budget or a target. An owed profile question waits for a
later pause (A-144), and the updates offer for a reply with no other question (`updates-offer.md`). The standard stands
until she answers; a "not now" is kept, dated, and asked again only after a week (`goals.md`).
