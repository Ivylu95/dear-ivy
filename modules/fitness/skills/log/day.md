# Showing the day

**Read when:** she asks how her day looks or what to eat or take next, after the first item
logged in a chat, or when a nutrition reminder goes (the reminders job reads this file). Part of
[`SKILL.md`](SKILL.md): all its rules hold, its ~ rule (card A-145) among them. A fasting window
also reads `fasting-window.md`. **Reads** also `data/fitness/supplements.md`, `usual_meals.md`,
`data/fitness/training.md`, `data/PROFILE.yaml`, [`../../knowledge/practices.md`](../../knowledge/practices.md).

"How am I doing today?" on a day she is logging food is this summary, and only it: no remark on
the hour, the day's feel, or how she is. Short, in order:

**Worked out the same way every time** (card A-044): every nutrient's standard is its daily need
in `practices.md` for her age and sex from `data/PROFILE.yaml` (her country's guideline where it
carries one), water included, or the label Daily Values there (2,000 kcal reference) where age
or sex is not known; a target she set under **Targets** overrides it for that nutrient only
(card A-043). That, minus what she has logged today (the source of truth: food, drink and
supplements taken), is what is left; everything below comes from it.

1. **Goals:** each target she has set, with a bar of ten (▰ done, ▱ to go) and the percentage.
   Calories are **net** (card A-048): eaten minus her logged exercise (the **Activity** line),
   one short line under the bar giving both, `eaten ~2,100 − burned 250`; a machine's or
   watch's figure used as given, marked as its figure. No figure logged: net is what she ate,
   `eaten ~1,800 · no exercise figure`, and no burn is estimated, not even a resting one.
   The next cell names the nutrient beside its amount (card A-145), by the target's **Kind**
   (card A-043): a **budget** (a ceiling she may stay under) reads `1,200 kcal room` or
   `400 mg sodium room`, and nothing ever pushes her to fill it; a **target** (to hit, say when
   gaining weight) reads `1,200 kcal left`; a **minimum** reads `45 g protein left`,
   `1.5 L water left`, `15 g fibre left`. Past 100% the bar is full and the cell says by how
   much, plainly, never as a verdict. With any goal set, only her goals get a line, calories
   included, none against the standard; with none set, `no-targets.md`. Never a nutrient
   shown, or totalled in the day file, with nothing to measure it against. No line under the
   table restates a figure ("~23 g so far, all from breakfast"); the only one is net calories.
2. **What next, in one closing paragraph** (cards A-044, A-045): plain prose, no list, no emoji
   of its own; asked in the chat what to eat or take next ("what should I have with dinner?"),
   the answer is this same paragraph, the food then any supplement, never split into parts
   under emoji of their own.
   - **Food first.** Opens by naming the goal it serves ("For the 97 g protein left: …"). One
     or two foods or meals for her next meal that close what is left inside her budget,
     preferring one that also fills another gap today, without listing the gaps; weighted
     toward her usual meals and the last two weeks; a common local choice only when her history
     has nothing that fits. None past a budget's room, not even as her call; little room left:
     say so plainly, suggest only what fits, however small. Each food with what it gives toward
     her goals (calories only where they are one, or none are set); never "you should" or "the
     gap". Each item claims only what it gives (card
     A-145): water covers water, never "the rest".
   - **A meal not logged changes this** (card A-100): nothing is closed or fitted, and what
     is left is "not counting lunch", never "before lunch" (`day-gaps.md`). A meal has passed
     only by her time ("this morning", "it's 4pm"), never the machine's clock: breakfast
     "this morning" leaves nothing passed, nothing asked.
   - **Then a supplement, only if one is needed** (card A-045), a top-up, not a course: by
     what each on her list carries (**Labels and packs**), one only when a need stays unmet after
     what she ate and the next meal suggested, and it would fill it (`fitness:supplements`,
     *Food first*); when food covers the day, nutrient supplements go unmentioned. Herbal and hormonal ones, which food does not stand in
     for, are said at the time and on the conditions her list gives ("the herbal one, if
     dinner's heavy"), any ⚠️ flag carried. **Her rules** in `data/fitness/supplements.md`
     are read first; none is named outside them. Food and every supplement come in this one
     update (card A-099); a separate supplement reminder is offered to no one who has it.

**Shape, in both views** (owner's ruling, 24 Oct 2026): tables of three short columns that fit a
phone without sideways scrolling, one emoji leading each row, ~ on estimates:

| | Progress | Left |
|---|---|---|
| 🔥 Calories | ▰▰▰▰▰▱▱▱▱▱ 48% | 1,250 |
| 🥩 Protein | ▰▰▰▰▰▱▱▱▱▱ 49% | 77 g |

Cells stay under about 16 characters; a longer note goes in one line under the table. This
replaces the bold-led list FIT-001 gives narrow chats, for this summary only. Bars and
percentages, never streaks, points or badges. Anything more only when she asks
(`day-detail.md`).
