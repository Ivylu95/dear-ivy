# Changing the list

**Read when:** she adds, changes or stops a supplement, sends a photo or list of them, or one
comes up on or past its pack's re-check date. Part of [`SKILL.md`](SKILL.md), whose rules all
still hold; one level deep. A changed list is then shown as `SKILL.md`'s table says.

- A new supplement, a change, or a photo of a list: update the file, then show
  the new list in the chat's view. A new one with no brand or label waits first (*Brand and
  label*, below); say in one line that it is kept and waiting for them. A photo is read row by row; anything unreadable is asked, never
  guessed. A label in another language: name and figures as printed, any translation marked as
  yours, and asked where unsure.
- Stopped one: move its row to the **Stopped** section with the date. Never delete.
- Add a dated line under **Changes** for every change, in her words.

## Brand and label

Every supplement is kept with its brand and its label, under **Labels and packs**: brand and
product, pack size, the label's serving and every figure per serving, her usual amount, the
date the pack started and the re-check date.

- **Never on the list without them** (card A-017). A supplement she names with no brand or
  label ("I've started vitamin C, 1000mg a day") is not added to **Current**: it goes under
  **Waiting for brand and label** with her words and the date, and the same reply asks, as its
  one question, which brand it is and for a photo of the front and the label. Once they come,
  it moves to **Taking** with its entry under **Labels and packs**. If she says she can't get
  them, ask once more on a later day it comes up; her no is kept in her words, and it stays
  waiting. Nothing is filled from a product she has not shown.
- **Re-check date** = pack started + pack size ÷ her usual amount a day. Where how often she
  takes it is not given, the date is "not known" and no re-check is asked.
- **Around the re-check date**, the next time it comes up, ask once: "Still the same
  <product>?" Yes: the pack starts that day. No: ask for the new front and label, and move the
  old entry under **Earlier labels** with the dates it was in use. Never asked twice for one
  pack.
- How full a bottle was when first recorded is asked only if she offers it; otherwise the
  pack counts from full.
