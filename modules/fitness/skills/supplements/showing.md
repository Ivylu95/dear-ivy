# Showing the list

**Read when:** she asks to see her supplements, or what she takes today, and after any change,
when the new list is shown. Part of [`SKILL.md`](SKILL.md), whose rules all still hold; one
level deep, nothing further to open.

Two views of the same list, **narrow (mobile)** and **wide (desktop)**,
following the chat's view (FIT-001 in `CLAUDE.md`): if the chat has none yet,
show narrow and ask once, last, which she is on. Either way, her own words in
each line; don't rename or "improve" anything.

**Narrow** (no table), grouped by when she takes them, in this shape:

**🏋️ Training day**
- **Morning:** <supplements> (<food note where the list has one>)
- **Pre-workout:** ...
- **Post-workout:** ...
- **Night:** ...

**😴 Rest day**
- **Morning:** ...
- **Main meal:** ...
- **Any time:** ...
- **Night:** ...
- **Skip:** <any marked Skip>

Times follow the list's own words, in the order of a day; a time with nothing in
it is left out. Food notes ride in brackets: "with food", "with food, crucial";
"Optional" and "N/A" are left out.

**Wide** is the list's table as kept, built by the principles below.

### The table's principles

1. **Complete.** Every supplement the file holds appears, grouped under
   **Taking**, **On hold** and **Not yet sorted** (named but not yet placed),
   in that order. **Stopped** ones appear only when she asks for them.
2. **Fixed columns, always in this order:**

   | Supplement | For | Dose | Training day | Rest day | With food? | Note |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

   **For** is what she takes it for, in her words. **Note** holds a hold, a
   doctor check (⚠️) or her own condition on it ("only on weekdays").
3. **Ordered by the day:** within a group, rows run morning to night by their
   first time, then by name.
4. **Nothing guessed.** A blank shows as "not given yet", never filled from
   general knowledge or a label she has not sent.
5. **Flags stay visible.** Anything marked to check with a doctor or
   pharmacist carries ⚠️ in **Note**, in both views.
6. **Her words.** Each cell as the file keeps it; nothing renamed, improved or
   advised.
7. **One line under it** names what is still missing ("Doses come with your
   photos"), and any waiting for a brand and label, and only when something is.

The narrow view carries the same groups, flags and "not given yet", as a
bold-led list.

- If she asks for today only, ask once whether it's a training or rest day, then
  show just that day's half, in the chat's view.
- Under either view, one line only if something on the list is marked as needing
  a check with her doctor.
- **End with one line on how to switch**, worded fresh, e.g. _"Say 'wide' for
  the full table."_ or _"Say 'narrow' if you're on your phone."_
