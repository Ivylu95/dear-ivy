# First appointment

_Last updated: 2026-06-12_

## How it went

Better than she expected, which she said twice. Fifty minutes, mostly history.

He asked what she would call the last six months and she tried three things and
landed on **"the one I lost"**. That is now the phrase — see
`data/me/how_to_talk_to_me.md`.

## What he said

- Sleep would come back before the mood did, and not to read the gap as failure.
- Fortnightly once the first few are done.
- Did not put a name on anything, and she noticed and was relieved.

## What she didn't say

The error on the sample run in March. Went in meaning to and didn't.
