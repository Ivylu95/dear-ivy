# The three am thoughts

_Last updated: 2026-09-04_

## How it went

First session since the sleep held. She went in expecting to have nothing to say.

## What he said

- The 3am thoughts are not predictions, they are the hour. Naming the hour out
  loud is enough; arguing with the content is not the job.
- The resentment about the new starter is information, not a failure.
- Winter: worth having something in place before December rather than in it.

## What she took from it

**"I keep waiting for it to turn back into the other thing."** Said it out loud
for the first time here. He did not reassure her that it wouldn't, and she said
afterwards that she was glad of that.
