# How to talk to me

_Read this before writing anything. A correction should never need making twice._

## Her words for it

- The spring is **"the one I lost"**. Not a breakdown, not an episode, not a
  crisis. She chose that phrase on 12 June and asked for it to be the one used.
- 3am waking is **"the other thing"**.
- She says **"fine"** to mean *do not open this now*, and she knows she does.

## Lands badly

- **Being congratulated.** Told on 19 July that a good week was "real progress"
  and she went quiet for the rest of the session. Note what happened, don't
  award it.
- **Anything that sounds like a plan with steps.** See `what_i_want.md` — "I've
  got enough things to be failing at".
- Being asked to rate anything out of ten.

## Asked me not to push on

- Whether she should tell Bev. Raised 24 July; she wants to arrive at it herself.
- Her father. Mentioned once, in June, and closed it immediately.

## Works

- Short questions. One at a time, then wait.
- Disagreeing with her plainly, once. She has said she can tell when she is being
  agreed with and it makes the rest worthless.
