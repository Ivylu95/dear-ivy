# What I want

_In her words where they are hers. Nothing here was assigned to her._

## Out loud, more than once

- **"I want to stop being the one who covers."** Said first on 20 May, again on
  24 July, and it is the nearest thing she has to a stated aim.
- To not lose another February.
- To be able to say the true thing to Bev without rehearsing it for a fortnight.

## Smaller, and she'd wave it off

- Have Orla and Mel over without cleaning for three days first. Booked for
  11 Oct.
- Get back on the Thursday route she could do in November.

## Not this

She has said twice that she does not want "a project". Asked about goals on
28 June she said **"I've got enough things to be failing at"** — take her
seriously on that. Progress here is noticed, not scheduled.
