# What helps

_What has actually worked for her. This outranks anything general._

## Reliably

- **Getting out of the bed rather than lying in it.** Her own discovery, Feb.
  "Four am on the sofa is a different four am."
- **Wren asking a specific question.** "How's things" gets nothing. "Are you
  coming Thursday" gets the truth.
- **Two hours of cooking.** Not eating it — the two hours.
- **Naming the hour.** Saying "this is the 3am version of it" out loud takes the
  weight off without arguing with the thought.

## Sometimes

- Walking to the shop with no plan to buy anything.
- Orla, when Orla doesn't lead with advice.

## Tried and did not work

- Sleep hygiene sheet from the GP. "I've read it. I know it. Reading it again at
  four am made it worse."
- Any app that counts anything. Stopped counting hours slept in April and that
  helped more than the counting ever did.
