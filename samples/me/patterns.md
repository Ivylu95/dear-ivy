# Patterns

Loops she has named herself. Nothing here was assigned to her.

## Volunteering before anyone asks

**What it looks like:** Hears there is a gap and fills it before the question
reaches her. Both lab rotas started this way.
**What sets it off:** Someone else looking stretched.
**What it costs me:** "By the time I notice I've agreed, it's a thing I'd be
letting someone down by taking back."
**What's helped:** Saying "let me look at the rota and come back to you" — a
sentence she and Wren worked out, not one offered to her.

### Examples
- **3 Nov 2025** — took the second rota before it was advertised.
- **19 Dec 2025** — Christmas ward cover, same week the sleep went.
- **14 Sep 2026** — offered to train the new starter on her day off. Caught it
  herself this time and un-offered it the next morning.

## Going quiet at exactly the point it would help to talk

**What it looks like:** Stops answering messages in the week she most needs the
person. Nine days of silence after Orla's birthday.
**What sets it off:** Having something to apologise for.
**What it costs me:** "It turns one thing I did into a second thing I did."
**What's helped:** Orla turning up rather than messaging.

### Examples
- **28 Feb 2026** — missed the birthday, nine days of nothing.
- **22 Mar 2026** — Orla came to the flat and it broke the silence.

## Exceptions

- **3 Sep 2026** — Orla asked how she really was and she answered properly, first
  time without being cornered into it.
