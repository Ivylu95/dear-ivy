# Orla

**Who they are to me:** My sister. Two years older, twenty minutes away.
**Phone:** 9000 0022

## The shape of it

Lives with her partner Mel. Sunday phone call, which Juno sometimes lets ring
out and then feels worse about than the call would have been.

Orla is the one who turns up rather than messages. That has been the thing that
worked twice now.

## What's good

- She didn't say anything about the state of the flat on 22 March, and Juno has
  mentioned that three times since. It is the single most-referenced kindness in
  the record.
- She asks the real question if you give her a second go at it.

## What's hard

- Leads with advice. Juno's words, 3 Sep: **"she solves me."**
- The missed birthday on 28 Feb is still sitting there unaddressed on both sides.

## Where it stands

Better than it has been. Juno answered properly on 3 September and has Orla and
Mel coming to the flat on 11 October — the first time since March.
