# Wren

**Who they are to me:** Closest friend. We climb on Thursdays.
**Phone:** 9000 0011

## The shape of it

Five years, all of it through the wall. Not someone Juno talks to about feelings
directly; someone she talks to *while doing something else*, which turns out to
be the format that works.

## What's good

- Asks specific questions. "Are you coming Thursday" gets an honest answer where
  "how are things" gets nothing — this is written up in `data/me/what_helps.md`.
- Made the GP appointment a condition of leaving the flat on 2 April. Juno is
  ambivalent about that and has said so; see `data/state/open_loops.md`.

## What's hard

- Juno lied to her for three months about why she stopped coming, and has not
  fully let that go.

## Where it stands

Back on Thursdays since 23 August. Juno on the first week back:
**"I was terrible and it was great."**
