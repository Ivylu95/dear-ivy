# Orla asked twice

_Last updated: 2026-09-02_

She asked how I was and I said fine, and then she left a gap and asked again,
properly, and I told her.

Told her about February. Told her the flat had got bad and that's why I kept
saying don't come over. Told her the birthday wasn't that I forgot.

She didn't solve me. She said "I knew some of that" and then she was quiet for a
bit and then she asked if her and Mel could come for lunch.

I've spent seven months assuming the conversation would cost me something.
