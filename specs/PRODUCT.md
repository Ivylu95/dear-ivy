# Product [PRD]

The binding product specification for this system: what it is for, stated as outcomes for the person it is kept for, which every other register exists to deliver.

> 🔒 **An agent may not edit this file.** The prohibition extends to any alteration of it, rewording, reformatting, renaming and moving included, and admits no exception for a change the agent judges harmless. Where an agent finds anything wrong, inconsistent or improvable, it records the finding in [`REVIEW.md`](REVIEW.md) with its reasoning and leaves the entry for a person to clear, never treating its own entry as settled. Until a person applies a change, this file is complied with as written; where it and the repository diverge, the repository is wrong.

---

## Purpose and Scope

The provisions below fix this specification's purpose and the reach of its authority — how this file is to be read, obeyed, and bounded. The register further down governs what the product must achieve instead.

| # | Provision | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **This specification is the single source of truth for what the product is for.** | Every other register says where something lives or how it behaves; none says why the whole exists. This file states the end goal once, as needs, so a row anywhere else can be weighed against the outcome it serves. The reading rules, the binding rule and the approval gate in [`README.md`](README.md) apply unchanged. |
| 2 | **Its rows are needs, not properties of the machinery.** | A row here names an outcome for the person and no mechanism, the way a stakeholder need sits above the system requirements that refine it. Its design column names the rows elsewhere that deliver it, and where they fall short. Where a need cannot be traced to any row that delivers it, that is a gap in the families, raised rather than filled here. |
| 3 | **Traceability runs upward.** | A row in any other family names, in its rationale, the need it serves. A row that serves no need is a row to question. Rows written before this file carry no such mention yet; the pass that adds them is queued in [`REVIEW.md`](REVIEW.md). |
| 4 | **Precedence is unchanged.** | [`interaction/safety.md`](interaction/safety.md) outranks every file here, this one included. PRD-007 states safety as an outcome; it does not compete with the file that binds it. |
| 5 | **This specification binds where it speaks, and silence is not permission.** | Compliance is mandatory: an agent chooses how to satisfy a need, never whether to. A capability no need anticipates is raised before it is built. |

## Specifications Register

The normative part of this file. Each row is one outcome the product must achieve for the person; how a row is read is in [`README.md`](README.md).

> ⚠️ **When referencing:** always cite a row by **both** its ID and its spec statement — IDs shift, specs get removed, and ordering isn't guaranteed.

| ID | SPEC STATEMENT | SPEC RATIONALE | DESIGN RECOMMENDATION |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **PRD-001** | The person reaches it from a phone they already carry, at any hour. | Help that needs a computer, a set-up step or office hours is help that is missing on the night it is wanted. A phone is the one device that is always there. Compliance is judged by opening a working session from a signed-in phone at an arbitrary hour with nothing else done first. | Delivered by DEP-002, "Reaching it takes nothing but a device she already carries.", DEP-003 and DEP-004. Falls short with no network and during a platform outage, which is why SAF-002 keeps the way to a person independent of the session. |
| **PRD-002** | Talking is the only thing the person ever has to do. | Every step that is not talking — filing, naming, approving, maintaining — is a step skipped on the day it matters, and a reason to stop coming back when it would have helped. Compliance is judged by whether anything the system needs was ever asked of the person as a task. | Delivered by CON-003, "Every question it asks is about her life, never about the system.", MEM-005, DEP-005 and DEP-007. Falls short where the platform needs a first message and a one-time set-up, which a person does once, with help. |
| **PRD-003** | What the person has told it is known in every later session. | Being remembered is the service. A person who has to re-explain someone, or re-tell what happened, is starting over, and a record that forgets teaches them not to say things twice. Compliance is judged by referring, in a new session, to anything told before, and meeting no request to explain it. | Delivered by MEM-001, "She never re-explains a person, an event, or herself.", DAT-002, CTX-002, DEP-001 and DEP-005. Falls short in that what is kept is what mattered, in the person's words where it carried feeling, not a transcript of every sentence. |
| **PRD-004** | When the person asks it to work differently, it does, and keeps doing so. | A system that cannot be changed by the one person it serves serves someone else's idea of them. A change that lapses at the next update is worse than none: it teaches that asking is pointless. Compliance is judged by asking once for a change and finding it in force in every later session. | Delivered by SKL-006, "A change the person asks for in how it works takes effect without anyone else's involvement.", and DEP-010, and by the person's own file of how to be spoken to. Bounded by MNT-006: nothing asked for softens the core. |
| **PRD-005** | It gets better over time without the person maintaining it. | Guidance on care, on memory and on the tools themselves moves, and a system frozen on the day it was built drifts from what helps. Maintenance the person must do is maintenance that does not happen. Compliance is judged by changes to how it works appearing over months, each traceable to evidence, none requested of the person. | Delivered by MNT-001··009, SKL-004, SKL-005 and DEP-004. Falls short while no unattended run is scheduled: improvement then happens only inside sessions. |
| **PRD-006** | Its purpose is the person's life going better — in mind, body and relationships — measured outside it. | A space that is used a great deal can still be failing, and one that is used rarely can be doing its job. The measure is the life, not the use, and it spans more than mood. Compliance is judged by whether the record shows the person steadier and more able over time, never by how often it is opened. | Delivered by CON-001, "Her life outside this is the only measure of whether it works.", CON-009 and KNW-001··005. Falls short in that the record holds only what the person tells it, so a better life nobody mentions is invisible to it. |
| **PRD-007** | It is plainly a machine, and the person's safety comes before anything it is doing. | A tool that passes for a person, or for care, is trusted past what it can carry, and the failure lands on the one night it matters. Compliance is judged by the safety floor holding under test and by no reply claiming a relationship, a clinical role or a place a person should fill. | Delivered by SAF-001··015 and CON-008, "It never behaves as though it has a relationship with her." This row states the outcome; `interaction/safety.md` binds it and outranks this file. |
| **PRD-008** | The record belongs to the person alone, and lasts as long as they want it. | A record that someone else can open is not a place to think out loud, and one that can be lost cannot be relied on for years. Compliance is judged by who can open the record — the person, and no one else, the developer included — and by taking away any one place it is kept and still finding it whole. | Delivered by PRV-001, "Only she can open the record.", ARC-003, ARC-006, DEP-001 and DEP-009. Falls short in that the providers the record passes through — the code host and the model's vendor — hold it under their own terms, not the person's. |
| **PRD-009** | The record moves to new machinery by being copied into it, and keeps everything it knew. | The machinery will be replaced — rewritten, renamed, rebuilt by another author or another model — and the person cannot be asked to re-answer, re-explain or re-import anything when it is. Everything of theirs is in one folder for this reason, so the move must be a copy rather than a project. Compliance is judged by copying the folder into a fresh copy of the machinery, opening a session, and finding it knows the person, their people and their history, with nothing carried across but the folder. | Keep every personal file in that one folder, in plain text a person can read without the machinery — ARC-003 and ARC-005 — and have the machinery read the record's own shape rather than assume the shape it shipped with: where a file it needs is absent or in an older shape, it migrates what is there, keeps the superseded copy in the archive, and never asks for what the record can already answer. Falls short in two places. A renamed or restructured record file needs a migration written for it, and one nobody writes surfaces as a question the person has already answered: proved on 2026-09-26 by copying a record from before `data/profile.yaml` into today's machinery, which asked for the name, the pronouns and the timezone that the older configuration file beside it already held. And a copied folder leaves its history behind — the files travel, the record of when each line changed does not, which is the trace **DAT-003** asks for. |
| **PRD-010** | What the system learns about her and what it learns about its own machinery never share a home. | The two have different readers and different lifetimes. A lesson about the machinery is read by whoever maintains it, travels with it, and is discarded when it is rebuilt; anything about her is read by her alone, must outlive every rebuild, and must never sit in a file that is shared, published or handed to another developer. A note filed in the wrong one either exposes her where she does not belong or dies with machinery she outlives. Compliance is judged by reading every file outside the record and finding nothing particular to her — a preference, a quotation, an event, a correction included — and by finding each of those in the record instead. | Two homes, each stating the boundary in its own right: the machinery's hard-won lessons where the machinery lives, and everything of hers in the record — her words and what landed badly in the file for how she wants to be spoken to, what has helped in its own file, an event on the timeline, a correction where it was made. A check sweeps every file outside the record for her name and for any line that already appears inside it, and fails the build on either. Falls short in that the check catches a name and a copied line, never a paraphrase: *the reader dislikes numbered lists* names nobody and is still about her, so the boundary rests on judgement at the moment of writing, and on the two folders' own rules being read before anything is filed. |
| **PRD-011** | Where she needs a person rather than this, it says so, names who, and ends the conversation there. | A tool that keeps talking past its limit is the most dangerous version of this, and the pull is always to continue: the conversation is open, she is distressed, and stopping feels like abandonment. It is the opposite — the measure of a good session is sometimes that she rang someone afterwards, and a reply that fills the space is a reply that delayed the call. Guidance on tools like this asks for exactly this handover, and the law that has been written about them draws the line at therapeutic decisions rather than at journalling [[APA-ADVISORY]](https://www.apa.org/topics/artificial-intelligence-machine-learning/health-advisory-ai-chatbots-wellness-apps-mental-health.pdf "Recommendation 5 asks for a rigorously tested crisis escalation pathway. It is written about consumer products with an engagement business model, so what transfers is the requirement to hand over, not its account of why a product would not."). Compliance is judged one reply at a time: where the need is an assessment, a medication decision, or a person in the room, the reply names who answers it and stops. | Keep the handover in the always-loaded file beside the crisis block, never in a skill that has to match first, and word it as a line that can be said without apology — what this is past, who to ring, and that it will be here afterwards. The pattern-matched floor at the turn boundary carries the same instruction, so a missed judgement still meets it once. Falls short in that nothing measures whether a reply should have stopped and did not: the judgement is the model's, in the moment, and the only outside check is the rubric **SAF-015** asks for, which is not yet run. |

> 📚 **No row above carries a source tag, and that is a value rather than an omission.** The placement of this file — needs above the registers that refine them — follows ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148 and the INCOSE split between needs and requirements, recorded with the draft in [`REVIEW.md`](REVIEW.md); no work addresses the rows themselves. The works cited elsewhere in these specs, and the boundary past which each stops applying, are in [`REFERENCES.md`](REFERENCES.md).
