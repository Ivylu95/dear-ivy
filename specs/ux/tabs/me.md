# About Me [ABM]

The binding specification for the view over the standing files: who she is, what she wants, what helps, how she wants to be spoken to, and the patterns she has named.

> 🔒 **An agent may not edit this file.** The prohibition extends to any alteration of it, rewording, reformatting, renaming and moving included, and admits no exception for a change the agent judges harmless. Where an agent finds anything wrong, inconsistent or improvable, it records the finding in [`REVIEW.md`](../../REVIEW.md) with its reasoning and leaves the entry for a person to clear, never treating its own entry as settled. Until a person applies a change, this file is complied with as written; where it and the repository diverge, the repository is wrong.

## Purpose and Scope

| #   | Provision                                                       | What it means                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| --- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **This specification is the single source of truth for how the undated files are read back.** | These are the files that describe a person rather than record an event, which makes this the view where a machine's account of someone is most likely to be mistaken for the person. This file fixes what must hold against that. The reading rules, the binding rule and the approval gate in [`README.md`](../../README.md) apply unchanged. |
| 2   | **It governs the view and the single write the surface is permitted.** | What may be written into these files, and that a pattern is only ever one she named, is [`../../interaction/memory.md`](../../interaction/memory.md) (`MEM`). That the surface is read-only, and the one narrow exception to it, is [`purpose.md`](../purpose.md) (`DSH-001`, `DSH-002`). This file says where that exception may appear. |
| 3   | **It binds where it speaks and defers where it is silent.** | Compliance is mandatory: an agent chooses how to satisfy a row, never whether to. Silence confers no authority to invent a requirement and attribute it here.                                                                                                                                                       |

## Specifications Register

> ⚠️ **When referencing:** always cite a row by **both** its ID and its spec statement — IDs shift, specs get removed, and ordering isn't guaranteed.

| ID | SPEC STATEMENT | SPEC RATIONALE | DESIGN RECOMMENDATION |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **ABM-001** | The view says, where patterns are shown, that only ones she has named herself appear there. | A pattern assigned to a person is another voice telling them what they are, and it is the single most consequential thing this record could get wrong about her. The rule exists on the write side; stating it on the read side is what lets her hold the record to it — she can only notice a pattern she did not name if she knows none should be there. The test: the patterns section carries the claim in both its filled and empty states. | State it in the section's own empty text and in its standing note, phrased in her favour: none have been put there, and none will be. It works because the claim is made where the content would be rather than in a preamble she may not reach. Its limit: the view cannot verify authorship, so the statement is a commitment the write side keeps. |
| **ABM-002** | The files where getting it wrong costs her something are visibly distinguished. | Two of these are read before anything is written — what has actually worked for her, and what lands badly — and a surface that renders all of them identically hides which ones carry that weight. They are not more important than the others; the difference is what a mistake costs, and that is worth a mark. The test: those files carry a distinguishing mark on this view, and the mark does not read as a warning about their contents. | Mark them with a quiet glyph on the heading, drawn from the surface's own icon set. It works because the mark is a property of the file rather than of what is in it, so it is stable across every state the file has. Its limit: a glyph alone encodes the distinction in one channel, so it must not be the only thing carrying a meaning a reader has to act on. |
| **ABM-003** | Only sections with something in them are shown. | These files ship with headings and notes describing what belongs under each, and rendering the scaffolding shows her a record that looks fuller than it is. The cost is specific: a person reading their own record and seeing eight populated headings concludes the record knows them, and the conclusion is wrong. The test: a heading whose content is only the file's own note about what goes there does not render. | Test each section for real content at the read and render only those that pass. It works because the judgement is made once, where the file is parsed, rather than per view. Its limit: it cannot distinguish a genuine one-line entry from a scaffold note that resembles one. |
| **ABM-004** | What the surface calls her is shown and is not editable here. | A name is something she said, so it arrives the way everything she says arrives — she mentions it, and it is written down. Offering a field that does nothing is the version of this that reads as broken; offering one that works would spend the surface's single write exception on the wrong thing. The row exists because a name beside a picker is the most natural place in the whole surface to add an input. The test: the name is displayed with its source, and the page states in words how it changes. | Render it as content with its provenance shown, and say plainly that nothing on the page is edited by hand. It works because the explanation is adjacent to the absence, and an unexplained absence is what the next session fills in. Its limit: it does not restrict where the name is *used*, only where it may be changed. |
| **ABM-005** | The surface's one write is something she made rather than something she said, and it lives on this view alone. | The danger is the precedent, not the feature: the read-only rule's real protection is that there is nothing to argue about, and one exception spends it. Keeping the exception on the page about her — rather than beside a navigation row — also matters physically, because a file picker one mis-click from a nav item is a file picker opened by accident. The test: the whole of the surface's write capability is reachable from this view and from nowhere else. | Place the single control inside this page's own header, and keep its route, its module and its path singular as the surface-wide rule requires. It works because a reviewer can enumerate the write surface by opening one file. Its limit: this row places the exception; what the exception *is* remains fixed by the surface-wide rule and is not widened by anything here. |

> 📚 **No row above carries a source tag, and that is a value rather than an omission.** These registers govern one person's private record; where no published work genuinely addresses a row it stays bare, and reaching for a large name to decorate one is the failure mode — see **Source tags** in [`README.md`](../../README.md). The works cited elsewhere in these specs, and the boundary past which each stops applying, are in [`REFERENCES.md`](../../REFERENCES.md).
