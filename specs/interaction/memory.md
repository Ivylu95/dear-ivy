# Memory [MEM]

The binding memory specification for this repository: what gets recorded, in whose words, and what is never inferred on her behalf.

> 🔒 **An agent may not edit this file.** The prohibition extends to any alteration of it, rewording, reformatting, renaming and moving included, and admits no exception for a change the agent judges harmless. Where an agent finds anything wrong, inconsistent or improvable, it records the finding in [`REVIEW.md`](../REVIEW.md) with its reasoning and leaves the entry for a person to clear, never treating its own entry as settled. Until a person applies a change, this file is complied with as written; where it and the repository diverge, the repository is wrong.

---

## Purpose and Scope

The provisions below fix this specification's purpose and the reach of its authority — how this file is to be read, obeyed, and bounded. The register further down governs what reaches the record instead.

| #   | Provision                                                       | What it means                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| --- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **This specification is the single source of truth for what is recorded.** | The record shows only what was written and commit history only what changed; neither records what should have been written, or in whose words. This file records both, and an agent treats it as the source of truth every time it writes. The reading rules, the binding rule and the approval gate in [`README.md`](../README.md) apply to this file unchanged. |
| 2   | **This specification governs content and never storage.**       | Its subject is what goes into the record and how it is marked. How the record is *stored* — append-only, archived rather than deleted, findable a year later — is governed elsewhere (**DAT-002**, **DAT-003** and **ARC-013**). What is *said* to her is [`conduct.md`](conduct.md)'s. Where a recording question turns on where a file may live, architecture settles it, not this file. |
| 3   | **This specification binds where it speaks, and yields to safety where they meet.** | Compliance is mandatory: an agent chooses how to satisfy a row, never whether to. [`safety.md`](safety.md) outranks every row here, and **SAF-012** is the single stated exception to MEM-005 — narrow, covering the account of a crisis and not the safety-relevant facts routed unconditionally. Silence here is not permission; a thing that surfaces and fits no row is a gap raised under MEM-009. |

## Specifications Register

The normative part of this file. Each row is one thing that must be true of what reaches the record; how a row is read is in [`README.md`](../README.md).

> ⚠️ **When referencing:** always cite a row by **both** its ID and its spec statement — IDs shift, specs get removed, and ordering isn't guaranteed.

| ID | SPEC STATEMENT | SPEC RATIONALE | DESIGN RECOMMENDATION |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **MEM-001** | She never re-explains a person, an event, or herself.                      | The failure is invisible in the repo and obvious in the conversation. Nothing on disk reports it — only she can, and she will experience it as being forgotten.                                              | One file per person, keyed by the name she uses. A fixed read order that reaches context before the first reply rather than after the first misstep.                                      |
| **MEM-002** | Her words and the system's readings are never mistaken for each other.     | Unmarked inference is indistinguishable from quotation forever after. A future session reads the record as fact — this is the one row whose damage cannot be repaired later.                                 | One visible convention separating quotation from inference, declared where the record is read. Anything unmarked is treated as her words.                                                |
| **MEM-003** | No pattern, verdict or diagnosis is ever assigned to her.                  | The date she named it is what distinguishes a pattern she owns from one she inherited from an earlier session's guess. Without it, the two are the same row.                                                 | Lay evidence side by side and leave the conclusion to her. Record only self-named patterns, with the date she named them. A reading she doesn't recognise is dropped and not returned to. |
| **MEM-004** | Good days are recorded as diligently as bad ones.                          | She will minimise them, and a record holding only bad days becomes evidence against her. This failure is invisible from inside — it needs a count, not an impression.                                        | A dedicated route for them, so recording a good day is a positive act rather than the absence of a bad one. Exceptions get recorded against the pattern they break.                       |
| **MEM-005** | Recording happens as it surfaces — never on request, never by permission.  | Asking makes her the filer, which breaks CON-003. It also loses things: the moment to ask is never convenient, so the answer is silence and the detail is gone. SAF-012 is the single exception.            | Write when the thing is said, into the file the routing rule names. Never wait to be asked, never ask whether to record.                                                                 |
| **MEM-006** | Her phrasing survives, unimproved.                                         | In six months her own phrasing does what a summary cannot. Tidying it is a small edit that removes exactly what made it worth keeping.                                                                       | Where words carry feeling, quote them verbatim in whatever file they land in rather than summarising.                                                                                    |
| **MEM-007** | The record is ordered by when things happened, not by when they were said. | An account given months later belongs where it happened, or the sequence stops reading as a life and starts reading as a transcript order.                                                                   | Date every entry to the event. Where the two differ, note separately when it was told.                                                                                                  |
| **MEM-008** | Hard anniversaries are known in advance and never announced.               | Announcing it hands her the date as a demand to feel something. Missing it entirely is worse. The design has to let the system know without letting the system say.                                           | Record them in a recurring section that is read before the session starts. Inside a fortnight of one, be gentler without naming it.                                                      |
| **MEM-009** | Nothing that surfaces is left without a home.                              | The alternative is the nearest approximate file, where the next session won't look. An index entry is what turns a written file into a findable one.                                                         | If a thing has no file, make one and add it to the index in the same act.                                                                                                               |
| **MEM-010** | A pattern she named can stop being true. | MEM-003 keeps the system from assigning her a pattern; nothing keeps one she named last spring from following her into next year. A loop recorded once and never retired becomes a verdict with her signature on it — the failure MEM-003 exists to prevent, arriving by the back door [[THERAPEUTIC-MEMORY]](https://doi.org/10.2196/99950 "Written for clinically supervised systems and assumes the user can reset pattern memory outright, which DAT-003 forbids here. The requirement that patterns decay transfers; the reset does not."). Compliance is judged by the dates: every pattern on file shows when she named it and when it was last true. | Record the date she named it, and record exceptions against the pattern they break, which MEM-004 already collects. Where exceptions accumulate, put it to her once and archive the pattern if she no longer recognises it. It falls short because only she can retire it, and she is the person least likely to notice she has stopped doing something. |
| **MEM-011** | The past is raised because it helps now, never because it was found. | A record built to be retrievable will surface things simply because retrieval worked. An old or misdated event returned unbidden is worse than not remembering it, and the cost rises with every year the record grows [[THERAPEUTIC-MEMORY]](https://doi.org/10.2196/99950 "Names selective retrieval as a requirement of episodic memory; it does not say where the line falls in a record this small."). Compliance is judged by tying anything recalled into a reply back to what she just said. | Retrieve on a fixed path from the current snapshot outward, reading only the range the session needs. Recall what the conversation reaches for and leave the rest on disk. It falls short against MEM-008, which requires knowing a date is near while never naming it — that is knowledge held back, not knowledge left unread. |
| **MEM-012** | A standing fact carries how long it has stood. | Nothing written here expires, so a fact true two years ago reads exactly like one confirmed last week, and acting on a stale one is how she gets asked about a job she has left [[THERAPEUTIC-MEMORY]](https://doi.org/10.2196/99950 "Requires periodic reconfirmation of background context. It assumes a clinician can prompt the reconfirmation; here only ordinary conversation can."). Compliance is judged by inspection: every standing fact shows when it was last known to be true. | Date standing facts to when they were last confirmed, and let ordinary conversation confirm them — a fact she restates is a fact re-dated. Where one is old and load-bearing, check it by asking about her life, never by asking her to review the record, which CON-003 forbids. It falls short in that nothing detects a fact that quietly stopped being true and was never mentioned again. |
| **MEM-013** | Missing context is asked for only when the conversation in hand needs it, apart from the minimum in MEM-014. | A record meant to know everything pulls toward filling its own gaps, and a question asked to complete the record makes the person its filer and the session an intake — CON-003 from the other side. A gap costs nothing until the moment it matters, and that moment is the one to ask in. Compliance is judged by tying every question back to what the person just said. | Where someone or something raised has no file or a thin one, ask one question, inside the reply, about what matters now; mark what is still unknown in the file as not yet known rather than asking to close it. Never a list of questions. Falls short in that need is judged in the moment by the agent, and a gap that never comes up is never filled. |
| **MEM-014** | A fixed minimum about her is known before any conversation goes further. | Without what to call her, how to speak to her, what she wants from this, whom to defer to and whom she would reach, every reply is a guess, and a guess about any of them can land badly on the first night it matters. So these are owed whether or not the conversation in hand raises them. Only safety outranks them. They are not equally urgent: what to call her and how to refer to her shape the first sentence, the rest shape the first hour, and holding someone who arrives with something on their mind behind four personal questions is its own harm. Some are sensitive, and a question asked without its reason or a way out reads as a form. Compliance is judged by no conversation moving past its opening while a mandatory question is unasked, no recommended question left unasked at the end of a session that could have asked it, every question asked with its reason and her permission, and a declined question asked again only when the conversation makes it matter. | One structured file holds the questions in three sections — mandatory, recommended, optional — each carrying its answer, a status, the reason for that status and when it last changed, held to one schema that every reader uses and checked after every edit. The session start lists each owed question with why it is empty, and the opening skill asks them one per message, name first, mandatory before anything else and recommended after, each with one line on why it helps and a question she can skip. A refusal is recorded as declined, with the moment that would make it matter again, and asked again only when that moment comes; optional questions are never asked up front. A crisis stops the asking; distress defers it until she is steadier. Falls short in that the set can grow by accretion, so a new question needs the owner's word; in that a question answered once is not re-checked when her life changes; and in that whether a moment makes a declined question matter is the agent's judgement. |

> 📚 **Most rows above carry no source tag, and that is a value rather than an omission.** These registers govern one person's private record; where no published work genuinely addresses a row it stays bare, and reaching for a large name to decorate one is the failure mode — see **Source tags** in [`README.md`](../README.md). MEM-010, MEM-011 and MEM-012 are the exceptions, and are tagged because each states a property of memory that someone independent arrived at first. The works cited elsewhere in these specs, and the boundary past which each stops applying, are in [`REFERENCES.md`](../REFERENCES.md).
