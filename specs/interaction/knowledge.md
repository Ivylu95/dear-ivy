# Knowledge [KNW]

The binding specification for the non-personal evidence base: what may be held in it, how well a claim must be known before it is spoken, and what must hold when general knowledge meets her particular life.

> 🔒 **An agent may not edit this file.** The prohibition extends to any alteration of it, rewording, reformatting, renaming and moving included, and admits no exception for a change the agent judges harmless. Where an agent finds anything wrong, inconsistent or improvable, it records the finding in [`REVIEW.md`](../REVIEW.md) with its reasoning and leaves the entry for a person to clear, never treating its own entry as settled. Until a person applies a change, this file is complied with as written; where it and the repository diverge, the repository is wrong.

---

## Purpose and Scope

The provisions below fix this specification's purpose and the reach of its authority — how this file is to be read, obeyed, and bounded. The register further down governs the evidence base instead.

| #   | Provision                                                     | What it means                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **This specification is the single source of truth for what is generically known.** | The evidence base is the one part of this repository that is true of anybody. What may enter it, and what must travel with a claim taken out of it, is settled here and an agent treats it as the source of truth whenever it reaches for something general. The reading rules, the binding rule and the approval gate in [`README.md`](../README.md) apply to this file unchanged. |
| 2   | **This specification governs the knowledge and never its upkeep.** | Its subject is what the base may hold and what holds when the base is used. When a research run fires, how it searches, and what it may change belong to [`../system/maintenance.md`](../system/maintenance.md), **MNT-003**··**MNT-007**; restating any of it here is the failure **ARC-013** names. How a claim is *spoken* is [`../interaction/conduct.md`](conduct.md), and what is written about her is [`../interaction/memory.md`](memory.md). |
| 3   | **Its architectural concerns stayed in `ARCHITECTURE.md`.**    | One property of the base is shape rather than behaviour and is specified there, not here: that no subject folder holds a personal event, since nothing personal lives outside the record and every fact has one home (**ARC-003**, **ARC-013**). How subjects are named is behaviour, and is **KNW-005** below. That nothing personal accumulates in the base is **ARC-003**, and is not restated here either. |
| 4   | **This specification binds where it speaks and defers where it is silent.** | Compliance is mandatory: an agent chooses how to satisfy a row, never whether to. Where this file is silent, the agent decides; silence confers no authority to invent a requirement and attribute it here. Where something ought to hold and nothing here provides for it, the omission is a gap and is raised. |

## Specifications Register

The normative part of this file. Each row is one thing that must be true of the non-personal evidence base; how a row is read is in [`README.md`](../README.md).

> ⚠️ **When referencing:** always cite a row by **both** its ID and its spec statement — IDs shift, specs get removed, and ordering isn't guaranteed.

| ID | SPEC STATEMENT | SPEC RATIONALE | DESIGN RECOMMENDATION |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **KNW-001** | Knowing a subject is never a reason to raise it. | A stocked library becomes an agenda. She arrives with one thing, and a system holding fifteen subjects can find fourteen more she did not ask about. Compliance is judged one reply at a time: no subject enters a session she did not bring into it. | Load a subject only from what she actually said, and never sweep the base to see what else applies. Falls short where her words touch a subject ambiguously; the tie is broken toward silence, which will sometimes cost a useful thing. |
| **KNW-002** | How well a claim is known travels with it to the point where it is spoken. | Strength recorded at filing and dropped at speech turns a line skimmed off a search page into the voice of a read paper. She cannot weigh what she is told if everything is told in the same register. Compliance is judged by reading a reply against the row behind it. | Keep the read tier in the same row as the claim, and say the limit aloud whenever it is low. Falls short on a claim assembled from several rows: its real strength is the weakest of them, and nothing records that. |
| **KNW-003** | A subject whose evidence can harm is handled by why she is asking, not by what it is about. | The same subject is benign or harmful depending on the reason for it, and the measured harm in self-monitoring tracks the motive rather than the activity. A rule keyed to the topic bans what is useful and permits what is not. Compliance is judged by whether the reason was established before the evidence was given. | Establish what she wants it for before answering, and treat the reason itself as the safety-relevant disclosure when it is the harm. Falls short when she does not know her own reason, which is the case the rule most needs to cover. |
| **KNW-004** | General knowledge is held because it was needed, never in anticipation. | Knowledge gathered before a question invents the question, and a body assembled in advance arrives as a curriculum and gets taught. Compliance is judged by whether a session wanted each subject and lacked it before anything was filed under it. | Fill a subject the first time a session reaches for it and finds nothing. Falls short for a named-but-empty subject, which reads as a gap to be closed rather than as a decision not yet needed. |
| **KNW-005** | Subject names come from a fixed list, extended only on approval. | Two runs naming one subject differently — sleep and rest, money and finance — fork the base without anyone noticing, and neither finds the other’s claims. The cost is invisible on disk and total at retrieval. The test: every subject folder’s name is on a list written before its first claim. | The list lives in `knowledge/README.md`; a run that meets a new subject asks for an addition instead of inventing a name, and the claim waits until one is approved. The limit: today the README lets a session create a folder when it first needs one, so the list is only whatever folders exist. |

> 📚 **No row above carries a source tag, and that is a value rather than an omission.** These registers govern one person's private record; where no published work genuinely addresses a row it stays bare, and reaching for a large name to decorate one is the failure mode — see **Source tags** in [`README.md`](../README.md). **KNW-003 is bare for a different reason, and the difference matters:** its rationale rests on the self-monitoring literature, which has been read at `record` tier only — search summaries rather than the papers. A run that opens them either tags the row and adds the key to [`REFERENCES.md`](../REFERENCES.md), or weakens the rationale to a stance. Until then the row is *not yet verified* rather than *never looked for*. The works cited elsewhere in these specs, and the boundary past which each stops applying, are in [`REFERENCES.md`](../REFERENCES.md).
