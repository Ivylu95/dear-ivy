# core/

The core module's part of the record: the spine, working memory, who she is,
the people in her life, the journal and the calendar. What each standing file
holds is in the map one level up, [`../README.md`](../README.md).

| Folder | What it holds |
|---|---|
| [`state/`](state/) | Working memory: the current snapshot, the session log, open threads |
| [`me/`](me/) | Who she is, what she wants, what helps, how to speak to her, patterns she has named |
| [`people/`](people/README.md) | One per person: a page, or a folder once one page isn't enough |
| [`journal/`](journal/README.md) | Dated entries |
| [`calendar/`](calendar/) | What is coming, and the dates that land hard every year |
| [`assets/`](assets/README.md) | Files that are not words |
| [`setup/`](setup/README.md) | How this copy is set up: where its harness releases come from |
| [`dev-chats/`](dev-chats/README.md) | The developer's words about his own life from development chats, moved out of the development record |

`timeline.md`, beside these, is the spine: every dated event, in order.
