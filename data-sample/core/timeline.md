## Eras

**Late 2025 — holding it together on the outside.** Working doubles through the
staffing gap and calling it temporary. Sleep goes first. Nobody at work knows,
and that is deliberate.

**Q1 2026 — the drop.** The gap doesn't close. Stops going to climbing, stops
answering Person-O. The flat gets bad. This is the quarter she later calls "the one
I lost".

**Q2 2026 — asking.** Sees a GP in April after Person-W pushes. First appointment
with Person-H in June. Nothing improves yet; she starts telling the truth out
loud, which is the change.

**Q3 2026 — slow, real ground.** Sleep comes back before the mood does. Two
shifts dropped. Climbing again, badly, which she finds funny.
