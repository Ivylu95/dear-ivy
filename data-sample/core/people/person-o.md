# Person-O

**Who they are to me:** My sister. Two years older, twenty minutes away.
**Phone:** 9000 0022

## The shape of it

Lives with her partner Person-M. Sunday phone call, which Person-J sometimes lets ring
out and then feels worse about than the call would have been.

Person-O is the one who turns up rather than messages. That has been the thing that
worked twice now.

## What's good

- She didn't say anything about the state of the flat on 22 March, and Person-J has
  mentioned that three times since. It is the single most-referenced kindness in
  the record.
- She asks the real question if you give her a second go at it.

## What's hard

- Leads with advice. Person-J's words, 3 Sep: **"she solves me."**
- The missed birthday on 28 Feb is still sitting there unaddressed on both sides.

## Where it stands

Better than it has been. Person-J answered properly on 3 September and has Person-O and
Person-M coming to the flat on 11 October — the first time since March.
