# Letter from Person-E, postmarked 2 Jul 2026

_Transcribed by Person-J the same evening; the spelling is Person-E's._

> The garden here has sweet peas up the fence and the man in room nine says they
> are his but they are not. Are you still going up the wall on Thursdays. You
> were always a climber, you went up the dresser at four. Dont work so hard,
> nobody thanks you for it at the end.

**What Person-J said about it:** "Nobody thanks you for it at the end. She's said
that to me since I was fifteen and it's the first time I've heard it."
