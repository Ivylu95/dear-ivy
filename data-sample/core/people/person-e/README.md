# Person-E

**Who they are to me:** My grandmother, on our mother's side. Ninety-one, in a
care home by the river since last winter.

A folder rather than a page because of the letters: she writes on paper, one a
month or so, and Person-J keeps every one. Each is in [`letters/`](letters/), dated
by the postmark.

## What's good

- She asks about climbing by name. Person-J's words, 2 Jul: **"she remembers the
  wall better than she remembers me some days, and I don't even mind."**
- The letters. They arrive on the bad weeks more often than chance.

## What's hard

- Visiting means a whole day off, and for a year there wasn't one. The guilt
  about that is older than the burnout.
- Person-O goes more often, and neither of them says so.

## What I want from this

To go once a month without it being a debt paid off.
