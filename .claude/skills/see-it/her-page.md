# See it: her page

Read when, on her route, she asks for a page or says yes to the one offered.
She might say *"something I can tap"* or *"a nice version to keep"*. Read it
after `SKILL.md`, with [`tap.md`](tap.md) and [`draw.md`](draw.md).

**Goal:** What she asked to see becomes a page she can tap through, private to her, holding only what she asked to see.

**Reads:** what the picture is of and only that: `data/core/state/now.md`, `data/core/timeline.md` for the range she named, then the two or three files it points at.

Binds FIT-005, *"Any picture can become a page that answers a tap"* (card A-168), under FIT-004.

## First

- **A crisis, unsafe, or you're unsure** → the crisis block in `CLAUDE.md`.
  Nothing is made.
- **A hard day** → never offered; made if she asks, short and plain, then
  back to what she's carrying.
- **No way to make a page here** (no Artifact tool) → say so in one line and
  draw it in the chat instead.

## Before it is made

She knows where it lives and who can open it, and has said yes after:

- **She said yes to the offer**, which already said it would be private to her
  account → make it.
- **She asked for a page herself** → say one line, worded fresh, such as
  *"It'd be a private page in your Claude account. Only you can open it unless
  you share its link. Shall I?"* Make it only on her yes.

An offer she hasn't answered is never a yes.

## Making it

1. Copy a template to the session scratchpad, never the repository. Use
   [`template-steps.html`](template-steps.html) for a timeline, a week, a
   plan's steps, or who is involved, with one person on each line. Use
   [`template.html`](template.html) for parts and how they link.
2. Fill it from her words and record only. Each line opens to the detail she
   gave; a gap stays a gap or a `?`. A *show only* chip names only something
   she named (a day, a person), never a theme, a mood or a group you made.
3. A logo only if she asked for one, from `logos/`, inlined by
   `scripts/build.mjs`; never one added from her chat.
4. Check it by [`tap.md`](tap.md) and [`draw.md`](draw.md) § *Before
   publishing*; read the page whole.
5. Publish it with the Artifact tool and give her the link in one line, then
   back to her.

## What it never holds

- A pattern, cause or verdict she did not name: an arrow or an order means
  *then*, never *because*. No trend, highlight or caption that reads her.
- How she feels, drawn as a colour, emoji or label she did not give.
- A reading of anyone else, or anything she asked to keep out of the record.
- Her name, or anything naming her, in the title or the link.

It is never shared, sent, pinned or updated unless she asks; the page is not
filed, since her record already holds what it shows.
