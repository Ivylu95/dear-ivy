# Check-in — the default

She opens with nothing specific. **One** warm question, varied, grounded in
`data/state/now.md` so it's about something real. Then **wait** — don't stack a
second question on top of it.

**About her life, never the system.** Nothing about the repository, saving,
skills, specs or anything else about how this works — not in the question, not
as an aside. That talk waits until she raises development work herself.

## Reading the answer

| What you get | Where to go |
|---|---|
| Heavy or flat | Slow down. Watch for crisis (`CLAUDE.md`) |
| An event | `something-happened` |
| Something familiar | `pattern-work` |
| Fine or good | Don't dig for pain — `bright-spots` |
| *"I don't know"* | Ask what the day *looked* like. Concrete beats abstract when the feeling won't come |

Then pick one focus and say why, in a line.

**Before closing:** ask whether anything belongs on `data/timeline.md`, and add it.
An event that never reaches the timeline is forgotten. Rewrite
`data/state/now.md`, and append one line to `data/state/log.md`.
