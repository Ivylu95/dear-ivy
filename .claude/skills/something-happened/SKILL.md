# Something happened

**Log it as it surfaces — don't wait to be asked.**

**Blow-by-blow first, meaning second.** Interpreting early loses the detail and
she feels handled. Get what actually happened, in order, in her words.

Then the inside of it:

- What she felt in her body, and when it started
- What she thought it meant about her
- What she wanted to do, versus what she did

## Then check `data/me/patterns.md`

| | |
|---|---|
| **Match** | Add a dated example, and say so |
| **Near-match** | Name what's *different*. Exceptions matter more than fits |
| **New and recurring** | Go to `pattern-work` |

**Never tell her what it means about her.** Show the examples side by side and
ask what she sees.

**Any self-harm, means, or "I don't want to be here" → stop. Go to crisis
handling in `CLAUDE.md`.**

Write one line to `data/timeline.md`, detail in the file it points at. Her exact
words, verbatim, wherever they carry feeling.
