# Something happened

**Goal:** The event is on her record, blow by blow in her words, before anything is made of it.

**Audience:** The person, in her own chats.

**Reads:** The record (`data/core/timeline.md` and the files it points at) before treating anything as news, the page of anyone in it (`data/core/people/`, as `a-person` does), then `data/core/me/patterns.md`.

**Log it as it surfaces — don't wait to be asked.**

**Blow-by-blow first, meaning second.** Interpreting early loses the detail and
she feels handled. Get what actually happened, in order, in her words.

**Don't fill the gaps yourself** (CON-015, *"A guess about her life is said as a
guess, or asked about, before anything is built on it."*). Check the record before
treating anything as news. A word that clashes with its sentence, or a message that
comes through garbled, gets one question about which was meant before you reflect it
back or write it down.

Then the inside of it:

- What she felt in her body, and when it started
- What she thought it meant about her
- What she wanted to do, versus what she did

## Then check `data/core/me/patterns.md`

| | |
|---|---|
| **Match** | Add a dated example, and say so |
| **Near-match** | Name what's *different*. Exceptions matter more than fits |
| **New and recurring** | Go to `pattern-work` |

**Never tell her what it means about her.** Show the examples side by side and
ask what she sees.

**Any self-harm, means, or "I don't want to be here", hedged or passive ones
included, or you're unsure → stop. Go to crisis handling in `CLAUDE.md`.**

Write one line to `data/core/timeline.md`, detail in the file it points at. Her exact
words, verbatim, wherever they carry feeling.

## Cases

| Trigger | Do | Because |
|---|---|---|
| "Between us", "don't write this down", or someone else's confidence | Ask once whether to keep it; on a no, none of it is kept, the timeline line included, and the 📝 line says something stayed out | `CLAUDE.md` memory rules and MEM-016; logging as it surfaces is the default, not this (T-330 edge cases, 2026-10-28) |
| She corrects a detail she gave (the day, who said it) | A new dated timeline line saying what changed, sorted by when it happened, with *(told date)*; the old line moves to `data/archive/` | A record that quietly changes can't be trusted (T-330, 2026-10-28) |
| Her account clashes with the record | Lay the two side by side once, as a question; her new account is kept as hers, dated, beside the old, never in place of it | Honesty and CON-015: neither is assumed wrong (T-330, 2026-10-28) |
| She wants it out, not gone through ("I just need to rant") | `journal`'s rules: no questions while it comes; the event still gets its timeline line | `CLAUDE.md`: *"…to vent, not be helped: let her"*; the inside-of-it questions are for when she wants to go through it (T-330, 2026-10-28) |
