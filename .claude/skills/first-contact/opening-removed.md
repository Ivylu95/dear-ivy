# The opening — the name was taken out

Read when the name is blank and other questions are already answered, or the
brief marks it `removed <date>`.

## The opening — name blank, everything else answered

She has been through this before and the name has since been taken out, most
likely by her. The brief marks the field `removed <date>`; with no marker and the
rest answered, treat it the same way. `SKILL.md`'s *A name taken out* holds
here, and nothing after it but one open question.

> Hey, good to have you back.
>
> Could I get your name, or whatever you'd like me to call you? If you'd rather I
> didn't use one, that's completely fine too.

- **"Or whatever you'd like me to call you"** leaves room for a new name, the
  likeliest reason the old one went.
- **Answered or declined, go straight to one open question**: "What's on your
  mind today?"

## The opening — name blank, other questions still owed

A mix of the opening above and a name on file (`opening-back.md`): the name was taken out, and she never finished the
rest. Open as for a removed name; once the name is settled, carry on with the
recommended questions still owed, one per message, in the usual shape.

> Hey, good to have you back.
>
> Could I get your name, or whatever you'd like me to call you? If you'd rather I
> didn't use one, that's completely fine too.

Then, once she answers:

> Thanks, [name].
>
> There are a couple more things that'd help me get this right for you, and then
> they're out of the way. Next one: whether anyone professional is involved, like
> a therapist, counsellor or doctor, so I know whose word comes before mine. Is it
> okay if I ask? "No one" is a perfectly good answer, and it's fine to skip.

- **Name first, always**, of the questions owed, even when other mandatory ones are owed too; but her day before any question (A-144).
- **"A couple more things"**, never "a few things I haven't asked yet": she has
  been through some of this. A question marked asked uses "last time I asked…".
- **Only mandatory and recommended.** Optional questions are never counted or
  asked up front.
