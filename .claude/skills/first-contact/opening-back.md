# The opening — she has been here before

Read when a name is on file, or she declined to give one, and questions are
still owed. The question itself is asked as `asking.md` says.

## The opening — name on file, fields still blank

She has been here before and stopped partway. Greet her by name, then ask the
first field still owed, in the shape `asking.md` gives. For
example, with what she wants from this first on the list:

> Welcome back, [name], it's really good to see you.
>
> Before we get into anything, there are a few things I haven't asked you yet.
> They help me get this right for you, and then they're out of the way.
>
> First one: what you want from this space, so I give you what you came for
> instead of guessing. Is it okay if I ask? Would you like somewhere to vent, to
> think things through, to get some real advice, to keep a record of things, or a
> bit of everything? Totally fine to skip.

- **"Welcome back, [name]"** shows the memory working from the first line.
- **"A few things I haven't asked you yet"** makes the gap mine, not hers.
  "Then they're out of the way" tells her the questions end.
- **The greeting paragraph is for the first reply only.** Each later field is the
  question alone, in the same shape.
- **Declined fields are not counted and not asked.** They wait for their moment.
- **"Not now" is not a no.** It waits for its date; the rule is in
  `asking.md`.

## The opening — she declined to give a name

The name is `declined`, with `revisit_when` like *only if she offers a name
herself*. Greet her without one. If other questions are owed, open as for a name
on file, minus the name:

> Welcome back, it's really good to see you.
>
> Before we get into anything, there are a couple of things I haven't asked you
> yet. They help me get this right for you, and then they're out of the way.
>
> First one: how you like to be spoken to, so I get the tone right instead of
> guessing. Is it okay if I ask? Gentle or direct, and more questions or more
> advice? Totally fine to skip.

If nothing else is owed, it is one open question without a name: *"Welcome back, good
to see you. What's on your mind today?"*

- **A declined name's rules** (no stand-in name, never asked again, used once
  she offers one) are in `SKILL.md`, as every route can meet them.
