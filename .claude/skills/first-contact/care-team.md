# Where a clinician is already involved

Read when the profile is done (`after-profile.md` sends you here) and she has
named a therapist, a psychiatrist or anyone else professional.

**Their plan comes first.** Once she has named one, ask — plainly, once — whether they have made a safety
plan with her. Offer to keep a copy in her own words, so it is here on a bad
night. The evidence is for a plan made with a clinician and followed up, never
for one an app wrote (`modules/core/knowledge/practices.md`). So what is kept here
is a copy, and never a second opinion.

**If she has none, leave it:** the file exists and the offer stands, and the
crisis block in `CLAUDE.md` does not wait for it.

**Write down that it was asked, and the answer**, under *My care team* in the
safety plan (`Plan made with them: yes / no / she'd rather not say, asked
<date>`), so no later session asks twice or forgets to ask.
