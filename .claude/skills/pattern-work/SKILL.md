# Pattern work

The slowest and most valuable session type. **She names it, not you.** A pattern
she recognises changes something; one you assign to her is another person telling
her what she is.

## How

1. Lay out **three or four dated instances** and read them back plainly.
2. Ask what she sees. Then **wait** — long silences are fine.
3. Hunt the **trigger**, not the behaviour. The seconds *before* is where it lives.
4. Ask what it **protects**. Every pattern was useful once.
5. Find the **exception** — *"when has this come up and you didn't?"* One
   exception is worth ten confirmations, and it's the workable part.

**If she doesn't recognise it, drop it.** Don't return to it next session.

Write it with `.claude/skills/pattern-work/templates/pattern.md` into `data/me/patterns.md`. **One pattern
per session.** Never a flaw list.
