## Bringing her copy up to date

**Evidence first, never a guess at its version**: her doctor's output, whether `.claude/hooks/lib/update/update.mjs` exists, her own commits outside `data/`, and branches main lacks. A copy older than the doctor has none of its own. Run `port.md`'s read-only lines in her folder, names and dates only (the oldest commit is the copy's making). Once today's download is on her machine, run `SETUP_DOCTOR_ROOT=<her folder> node ux/scripts/setup-doctor.mjs --json`. The sheet's first reply names both moves.

**Before any route, in this order**:

1. **First, find her country's checked entry in the crisis-lines catalogue.** If none exists, stop, write nothing in her copy, and say the developer must add one from official pages.
2. **Her stranded saves are carried into main first**, or a conflict handed over, before any update or import reads or changes her copy. They are the branches main lacks in her doctor's output, carried on her machine by `repair.md`. Say plainly what is and is not lost. On a port, the import carries a conflicting branch, never a hand merge (`port.md`).
3. **Her own edits looked for** (her commits outside `data/`, the doctor's `claude-md`) and named: an update replaces them. Nothing overwrites them before she is asked, through the developer. Each stays recoverable (the updater's tag, the history, or a `local-` skill first). Which to keep is hers.

**Two routes.**

- **A signed release she accepts in her own chat**, where the layout is today's. With the updater, it is a release (`dev/skills/release/SKILL.md`, its repository set up first), signed with the key her `release-key.pub` already matches. She pastes its key and types its phrase. Where `.claude/release/release-key.pub` is missing, or no release source is pinned (`data/core/setup/release-source.json`, or `.claude/release/source.json` from before the address moved), every release is held back and a first install refused. The file is restored from her copy's own history as a new commit, never typed in or taken from a chat. With no updater and today's layout (no era so far, `copies.md`), it is the release skill's one-time first install. Its fingerprint goes to her directly, and every later release is checked against the key it installs.
- **A fresh copy with her old record imported** (`port.md`) fits an older layout, one changed by hand throughout, or a folder never on GitHub. That folder first gets her own repository, as hers. An older layout keeps `CLAUDE.md` inside `.claude/` (before 3 Oct), no `data/`, or a specs folder at the root. Its first install would leave the old development skills live, fail its own test in place, and be rolled back.

**Done** is `new-copy-close.md` § *Finish on evidence*, a port's step 12 first. A second session reads back a saved conversation, her doctor is clean on what was touched, and `update.mjs status` names the version (a new copy reads `v0` until its first release). Say what rests on her word alone, what is outstanding and which tokens expire. The helper's access is gone, the old repository's included (step 16). **Her second copy** comes level after the update by its scheduled pull, checked by its last log (`git -C <folder> log -1` matching main).
