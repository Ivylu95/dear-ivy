# Her side

**Read when** answering in her own chat.

**Her reply, every time: one plain line in her terms, nothing added** (any question inside it), then only `CLAUDE.md`'s closing block. No setup words (branch, workflow, doctor, token, repository, commit, push, Actions, a settings page). No advice she did not ask for, and nothing on how saving works.

**Upset about the missing thing itself, with no sign of risk: hear her, then look and carry in that same reply, never put off.** Her line says only that it is there now, and what is there, never how or why, no branch, saving or system. Any other repair waits while she is in distress; any sign of risk, the crisis block.

## How she is answered

The record is read in `CLAUDE.md`'s retrieval order before asking. What she says is missing may be there, or something she asked to keep out, and she is told so plainly. A setup note kept is named on the 📝 line in her terms (*📝 I've left a note so the setup side gets looked at*). A `FEEDBACK.md` lesson, or a question back mid-run, goes on the 🔧 line (`SKILL.md` § 6). A message that names no part (*"it's acting weird"*) gets a question about what she noticed, in her words, before any answer. Several causes: the longest-lasting the doctor shows for what she asked, and look no further. A setup lays the pipe and never speaks for her. An import, or coming back to a retired copy, is never run here: a setup note and one line.

Diagnosis is silent. *"My messages stopped"* can mean Telegram or saving: check both silently, answer the one that is off. Only two fixes are made, and only when the doctor proves them at once. The first carries a `carry` branch into main if it merges cleanly or its conflict lies inside her record only, settled by merging and keeping both sides as `CLAUDE.md` says. The second retries a failed push. For a carry, read `.claude/memory/unpushed-saves-may-already-be-safe.md` first, and its reset is never the agent's. Run `git merge --no-ff` only in a clean checkout level with origin's main (a fresh worktree in the session's scratchpad, never `temp/`), then push. A conflict in `PUSH_FAILED.md` takes main's side. Starting the workflow by hand never carries a branch. Anything else, a conflict outside her record included, becomes a note for the developer.

The one line, worded fresh; a carried save gets no 📝 line (nothing new about her):

| What happened | What she hears |
|---|---|
| A save was stranded, now carried | *"A couple of your chats hadn't finished saving. They're saved now."* |
| A stranded save that will not carry (a conflict outside her record), so what she said seems forgotten | *"What you told me is kept; it just hasn't joined the rest yet."* Never "nothing's broken"; what she was looking for may be read from that save and said back. Then the setup note |
| The check-in nudge switched off | *"It's switched off at the moment. Nothing's broken, and your chats are saving."* Never offer to turn it on (`CON-009`) |
| Paused by the safety rule | *"They'll start again tomorrow."* Lead with her, not the system; nothing about why; switched off outranks it |
| Nothing broken | *"Nothing's broken. Your chats are saving."* Then, if she was looking for something, what the record holds |
| Anything needing an account, a token, a settings page or a person | First *"Your chats are still saving."* when the doctor shows it, then *"Something on the setup side needs fixing."*; in a copy with no `dev/` (no one checks its notes unasked), *"Something on the setup side needs whoever set this up to look at it. Worth letting them know."*, naming them if the record does |

Never raise in her chat: setup questions (where she lives, which account), leftover branches, or any part she did not ask about. The crisis numbers check waits too, unless she says she has moved.
