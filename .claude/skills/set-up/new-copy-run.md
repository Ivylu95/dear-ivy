# Running a new copy

**Read when** a new copy is asked for, before its first step (`SKILL.md` § 3); a resume is [`remote-evidence.md`](remote-evidence.md)'s, the close [`new-copy-close.md`](new-copy-close.md)'s.

**The steps are in [`new-copy.md`](new-copy.md) (1 and 2), [`new-copy-download.md`](new-copy-download.md) (3 to 7), [`new-copy-finish.md`](new-copy-finish.md) (8 to 11) and `new-copy-close.md` (12 to 16), read fresh every run, never recalled** (`FND-005`). Their numbered headings are the steps, with step 15's items in [`telegram.md`](telegram.md) and [`telegram-routine.md`](telegram-routine.md). In a clone the download has not been run in, read none of its record (steps 3 to 5). Until step 12, whoever is typing is setting it up, and no profile question is asked of them (the brief's *NEW COPY*, then *SETUP NOT YET HANDED OVER*). The first conversation, on the phone, is hers. A rider inside a step (*no collaborators*, *two-factor on*) is a pass condition, never a line of its own. If a step file is missing or rewritten into something else, say so and stop. Where `dev/` exists, read `dev/skills/set-up/cases.md`. **An open case is a known hole, warned of before its step.** Those open for a copy made for someone else are named in one list.

## Before the first step

One question per message, in this order, each only if the evidence or the request has not answered it:

1. **Who is it for, which country do they live in, and where will it be set up?** Every account is the person's own (`PRV-001`). For someone else, every step is done **signed in as them, on their machine**, never a work computer, and the helper keeps no access (step 16). It also asks: **are they 18 or over, and have they agreed to it, knowing what it keeps?** On a no to either, stop (`policies/TERMS_OF_USE.md`).
   - **The country is recorded**, in the setup notes as `- country · <country>`.
   - **With no checked entry for it in `modules/core/knowledge/crisis-lines.md`, stop before her first conversation. Say the doctor reads the copy failed until the developer adds that country's numbers** from official pages (`SAF-016`). Singapore's sit in more places than her safety plan. Never a hand edit of `CLAUDE.md` or the safety hooks, never a number from memory.
2. **Is it part-built?** Nothing yet: from the top · partly: *Resuming* · a finished record holding someone's life: stop, and ask what they want changed.
3. **Walked or the sheet** (below).

## Walked, or the sheet

| Mode | When |
|---|---|
| **Walked** | Claude Code runs on the computer being set up and sees its results; a cloud session cannot reach it, so it is the sheet |
| **The sheet** | The setup happens on another machine or another person's accounts |

In the breadcrumb, ➖ is step 16 for a copy for the developer himself, and steps 3 to 8 apply only to the original, never-downloaded copy. Offer the sheet unasked when the request says the setup happens elsewhere. It is too long for a reply: a file in the session's scratchpad sent with `SendUserFile` (else a private artifact, or its path), with a three-line summary in the reply. It gives every step with its result, and for each command the shell, whose credentials it runs as, and which parts are placeholders. Nothing is marked from a sheet. Close a new copy by naming the two invisible steps (`new-copy-close.md`) and asking what the verification step printed. Close one part by asking what its *You should see* line showed.
