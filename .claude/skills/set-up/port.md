# Porting her copy into a new one

**Read when** another person's copy takes the new-copy route: an older layout, one changed by hand throughout, or a folder never on GitHub. **A copy only from before the updater** (*"onto the new harness"*, `CLAUDE.md` at the root, no `update.mjs`) **takes the release skill's one-time first install instead**; no era ever had that shape (`dev/skills/set-up/copies.md`). Its fingerprint is sent to her directly, and every later release is checked against the key it installs. A port runs from the developer's own development chat as the sheet, every step on her machine, signed in as her, never cloned here or access asked for.

**First, at every visit, her lessons come home.** Before any step, ask for her doctor's output, her `FEEDBACK.md` and her setup notes' `open` lines. Each general one becomes a case here.

**Resuming a port** (*"where did we get to?"*): its `port:` setup note is the plan, read, never asked about. Name each of its moves still owed, in its order, step 14 into a new folder included.

**Her country first.** With no checked entry for it in `modules/core/knowledge/crisis-lines.md`, stop before any route (no download, no import, no carrying of saves). Write nothing in her old copy, not even the `- country ·` line its doctor asks for. Say plainly that her copy in use still gives Singapore's numbers. The developer adds her country's entry from official pages, checked and dated, before the port goes on, never by a hand edit of `CLAUDE.md` or her safety plan.

**A stranded branch whose hand merge conflicts**: `git merge --abort`, pick no side, drop no line. On this route the import carries it, its inventory listing the branch's version of the file as its own `branch:` row.

**Her copy older than the doctor** (*Cannot find module*, no `FEEDBACK.md`): the reply names all three moves.

1. Read-only lines in her folder that print names and dates only: `ls -d specs dev CLAUDE.md .claude/CLAUDE.md FEEDBACK.md .claude/hooks/lib/update/update.mjs`, `git fetch origin`, `git branch -r --no-merged origin/main` and `git log --format='%h %ad %s' --date=short -- . ':(exclude)data'`.
2. Once today's download is on her machine, its doctor pointed at her folder: `SETUP_DOCTOR_ROOT=<her folder> node ux/scripts/setup-doctor.mjs --json`. **You should see** each part with its state, and nothing of her record.
3. Her own commits outside `data/` (the last line's output) are the old copy's lessons, read by their subjects, each general one a case here.

**Her old record comes in by the import** ([`import.md`](import.md)), its inventory and manifest, so nothing of hers is silently lost. The old copy is read only, and kept until a second session proves the new one. The import's commits are in before step 8, and a gate refusal at step 8 is `running.md`'s.

Both old names are taken, so the new repository (step 1) and the download's folder (steps 3 to 5) take new ones she chooses. The old ones are left as they are. To reuse the name, rename the old repository first, in its own Settings, signed in as her, the path from GitHub's page fetched now. Then run `git remote set-url origin <renamed>` in her old folder before any re-fetch. A new repository under the old name ends GitHub's redirect to it (GitHub docs, 2026-11-05). At the import, the new copy's setup notes get `- open · <date> · port: re-fetch the old repository before step 12; remove the app's access to it after the read-back, then fetch it a last time and run import step 4's check; step 14 into a new folder` and `- old copy · <where>`, so a resume and a retire both find them. The cut-over is also `new-copy-close.md` step 12's first lines. The import commits without pushing until step 8 adds the remote. So step 6 sees its carried answers, and step 8 more than one commit, none older than the download. Step 9 adds the new repository to the Claude app's access, and step 11 picks it on her phone. Just before step 12, in her new folder, whoever set it up fetches the old repository again. They import anything saved there since (import step 4's check, run again). The old repository is left as it is until a second session reads the new one back. Then the app's access to it is removed so no session lands there, and the note's last fetch and check follow. It is never archived before the app's access is removed; afterwards it is hers to keep or delete. A second copy cannot follow it, because the histories are unrelated and its pull stops on *Not possible to fast-forward*. So step 14 clones the new one into a new folder, its scheduled pull moved there, the old folder left to her. Never copy files over hers by hand. A key or phrase that reaches anyone but her, in her own chat, is not used: its issuer revokes it and sends another.
