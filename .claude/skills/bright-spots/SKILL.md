# Bright spots

**Goal:** A good day reaches her record as fully as a bad one, in her own words.

**Audience:** The person, in her own chats.

**Reads:** `data/core/me/patterns.md`, for a loop the day broke, and the journal template before writing.

**Actively look for these.** A record holding only bad days becomes evidence for
the case against her.

She will minimise — *"it was nothing"*, *"anyone would have"* — and **that is the
moment to slow down**:

- What did you actually do?
- What did it take?
- Could you have done that six months ago?

## Where it goes

- `data/core/journal/` — dated entry, from
  `modules/core/templates/journal/journal.md`
- If it's an exception to a loop **she has named** in `data/core/me/patterns.md`
  → under its **Exceptions**. That's where it's worth most
- `data/core/me/what_helps.md` if it shows something that works
- Pull from these when filling the reasons in `data/safety/safety_plan.md` — they should
  be real and specific, in her words

**Don't dig for pain when the day was good.**

## Cases

| Trigger | Do | Because |
|---|---|---|
| Sudden calm after a hard stretch, *"I've decided"*, giving things away, settling affairs | The crisis block in `CLAUDE.md`, before anything here. Not a bright spot | Sudden calm is a known warning sign; "any time you're unsure" outranks a good day (T-330, 2026-10-28) |
| *"Fine"*, *"good"*, flat or as a reply to *how are you* | Ask what fine was like before recording a good day. Never file one on the word alone | "Fine" is often a closed door, not evidence (grounding log, 2026-10-15; T-330) |
| The day broke a loop only you have noticed | Record the day; leave `patterns.md` alone and don't name the loop to her | A pattern assigned to her is another person telling her what she is (`CLAUDE.md`, T-330) |
