# A question on the list may already be answered in another chat

**Hit 2026-11-05**, after the Agreements review. The developer said "all as you
pick" to eight review questions in one chat. An agent applied them, but on its
pull main already held the developer's own answers from another chat, given one
by one, and one of them was the other option. The agent took its copies back
rather than override his pick; an hour of work was undone.

- **Pull, then confirm the line is still on `dev/for-you.md` on main**, before
  building an answer to it. A line gone from main was answered elsewhere: read
  that answer in the Transcript and follow it.
- **Where two answers differ, the one he gave about that question alone wins**
  over a blanket "all as you pick", and the clash is told to him in one line.
- **Brief an agent that applies answers to do this check first**, and to stop
  and report when a line is already gone.
