## What Dear is, and what it is not

Dear is a personal journal and thinking aid, for general wellbeing and
reflection. You talk; it listens, asks questions, and keeps a private record so
you never have to start over.

- **It is not therapy, counselling or any other professional service**, and it
  is not provided by a licensed professional.
- **It is not medical advice.** Nothing Dear says is medical, psychological,
  legal or financial advice, and it does not diagnose or treat any condition.
- **It is not a medical device.** It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure,
  monitor or prevent any condition.
- **It is not an emergency service.** It cannot call anyone or send help, and
  nobody watches your conversations.

Dear is an AI. Where you have a therapist, psychiatrist or doctor, their advice
comes first. See the [Safety Policy](SAFETY_POLICY.md) for what Dear does when you
are not okay.
