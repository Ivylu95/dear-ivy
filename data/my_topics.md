## 🧒 Preschool lessons (1.5–3 years)

**🍎 Healthy eating**

| Lesson | Activity |
|---|---|
| Rainbow Fruit Sort | Sort fruits by colour and count them |
| Hungry Caterpillar | Read the book, feed a box caterpillar |
| Every Day or Sometimes | Sort foods into 2 baskets |
| Big and Small Veggies | Touch and compare |
| Sticker Plate | Make a healthy plate |
| Pretend Market | Shop with toy food |
| Mystery Bag | Feel a fruit and guess |
| Grow a Plant | Green beans or spring onions in cups |
| Veggie Stamping | Paint with cut veggies |
| Tasting Day | "I like…" / "I don't like… yet" |

**🦷 Healthy habits**

| Lesson | Activity |
|---|---|
| Brush Your Teeth | Brush a toy's teeth, count to 10 |
| Drink Water | Pouring at the water table |
| Sleepy Time | Put dolls to bed, count down 5 to 1 |
| Move Your Body | Animal moves |
| Wash Your Hands | Song plus glitter "germs" |
| Cover Your Cough | Elbow cough, tissue in the bin |
| Tidy Up Time | Sort toys back into baskets |

**🥣 No-cook recipes**

Banana sushi · Yogurt cup · Mash it up (avocado or banana) · Toast faces · Fruit ice pops · Salad shake

**🎒 Take-home**

Snack mix bag · Overnight oats jar · Spring onion pot · Salt dough food (not for eating!) · Veggie print bag · Healthy plate mat

**⚠️ Safety:** quarter grapes and cherry tomatoes · no nuts, popcorn or hard sweets · check allergies · plastic tools only
